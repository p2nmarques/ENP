**Validation date:** 2026-08-23  
**Validation revision:** V3.4  
**Target:** ESP-IDF 6.0.2  
**Topology:** three ESP32 nodes — A/Gateway, B/failed next-hop, C/destination

## 1. Objective

Validate the frozen P4-E5D Step-3 orchestration adapter on real hardware,
demonstrating the complete transport-failure-to-route-repair path without
changing the Step-3 adapter or the RREQ/RREP wire format.

## 2. Hardware scenario

Initial route:

```text
A -> B -> C
```

Node B is then removed as the failed next-hop.

The intended repair path is:

```text
A -> C
```

This is the approved direct-alternative hardware gate. It validates real E5C
failure propagation, E5D repair activation, R4 discovery, RREQ/RREP exchange,
failed-next-hop replacement, and route installation without introducing a new
multi-hop repair requirement.

## 5. V3.4 validation correction

The V3.4 change was limited to the hardware-test assertion logic.

The previous polling assertion could miss the transient `STALE` state because
the real RREQ/RREP repair completed before the next observation sample.

V3.4 therefore accepts:

1. direct observation of `STALE`, or
2. evidence that the route transitioned into the E5D repair and has already
   completed the expected ACTIVE repair via C.

This is a validation-harness correction only. It does not modify:

- the Step-3 adapter;
- E5C;
- R4-A/B/C;
- the route table;
- the RREQ/RREP wire format;
- ESP-NOW transport behavior.

## 6. Freeze decision

All required Step-3 controlled and hardware acceptance criteria passed.

**P4-E5D Step-3 is HARDWARE VALIDATED and FROZEN as of 2026-08-23.**

The frozen baseline is the reference for subsequent development and regression.
Any future change to Step-3 requires a new revision and corresponding audit and
validation cycle.

## 7. Scope boundary

This record does not claim validation of:

- reliability recovery;
- application retransmission;
- multi-hop RREQ forwarding through a relay;
- multi-hop RREP forwarding through a relay;
- RERR generation/processing;
- unrelated future routing enhancements.

Those remain separate scopes.
